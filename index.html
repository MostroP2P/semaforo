      Monitoring

        How it works

        No server decides what you see here. A state comes from a signed event that your browser checks for itself, or from its absence: a Mostro that stops reporting turns red, never green.

        1. 1

          Every Mostro reports in

          Each monitored Mostro publishes a signed health event on Nostr at a fixed interval, every minute by default: its Lightning node, its payouts, its relays and its database.

        2. 2

          Your browser checks the signature

          This page reads those events straight from the relays and verifies every signature here, in your browser. A Mostro that goes quiet turns red.

        3. 3

          And an outside check

          A node on a stale chain may not know it, so its block height is compared with public block explorers. Falling behind turns it red.
